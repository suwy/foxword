About FoxWord

FoxWord is an interactive English learning app that helps users practice vocabulary, spelling, reading, and review activities through pictures and game-inspired learning experiences.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open FoxWord and use the Restore Purchases option. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

My purchase is not recognized. What should I do?

Please try Restore Purchases first. If the problem continues, contact us using the email address below.

How do I report a problem?

Please contact us by email and include a description of the problem and your device model if possible.

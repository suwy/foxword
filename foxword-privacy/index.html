Privacy Policy for FoxWord Kids

Last updated: September 30, 2026

FoxWord Kids is designed to help children practice vocabulary, spelling, reading, and review activities.

Information Stored on the Device

Child profile information, including nickname, age, learning progress, spelling history, review history, and learning reports, is stored locally on the user's device.

This information is not uploaded to our servers.

In-App Purchases

FoxWord Kids may offer an optional one-time premium purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

RevenueCat is used to manage and validate purchase status. RevenueCat may process purchase-related information necessary to provide premium access.

Advertising and Tracking

FoxWord Kids does not display third-party advertising and does not use personal information for cross-app tracking.

Children's Privacy

FoxWord Kids does not require children to create an online account. Learning data is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The app may use Apple App Store services and RevenueCat for purchase processing and purchase entitlement management.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. Any updates will be published on this page.
